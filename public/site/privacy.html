Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy explains how The London Wash (The Art of Laundry Pvt Ltd), Near Kerala PSC Office, Thazhe, Vettipuram, Pathanamthitta, Kerala 689645, India ("we", "us") collects and uses your information when you use our laundry and dry-cleaning services, our website thelondonwash.com, the London Wash app, or message us on WhatsApp.

Information we collect

  • Contact details: your name, mobile number, email address (if you give it) and pickup or delivery address.
  • Order details: the garments and services you order, garment tags and processing history, notes such as stains or damage, prices, payments and invoices.
  • London Wash Club: your membership tier, points, rewards and, if you share it, your birthday.
  • WhatsApp messages: when you message us on WhatsApp we receive your WhatsApp number, your WhatsApp profile name and the messages you send, and we keep a record of the messages we send you.
  • Website and app: basic technical information needed to run them securely (for example your browser type and the pages you open). We do not use advertising trackers on our website.

How we use it

  • To collect, clean, track and deliver your garments, and to contact you about your orders.
  • To answer your questions on WhatsApp, including automatic replies such as order status, prices, store hours and booking links, and to pass your chat to our team when you ask.
  • To send service messages you would expect, such as sign-in codes for our app, "your order is ready" and "out for delivery" updates.
  • To run the London Wash Club: points, tiers, birthday rewards and member offers. You can ask us to stop promotional messages at any time.
  • To keep accounts, invoices and records required by law, and to prevent fraud or misuse.

WhatsApp

We use the WhatsApp Business Platform provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. Messages you exchange with us are processed by WhatsApp/Meta under their own terms and privacy policy. We only message you on WhatsApp about your orders, your account, or offers you have agreed to receive. To stop WhatsApp messages from us, reply STOP or tell our team, and we will stop (except messages you request, such as sign-in codes).

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with service providers who help us run our business, and only for that purpose:

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform) for WhatsApp messaging;
  • Supabase (secure database hosting) and Vercel (website and app hosting);
  • our delivery staff, who see your name, number and address to complete pickups and deliveries;
  • authorities, where we are required to by law.

How long we keep it

We keep order and invoice records for as long as the law requires (normally up to 8 years for accounting records). WhatsApp message records are kept for up to 2 years. Club points expire as described in the Club rules. You can ask us to delete your account at any time (see below).

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop marketing messages. We will respond within 30 days. Some records (such as invoices) must be kept by law even if you close your account.

Delete your data

To delete your account and personal data, email hello@thelondonwash.com with the subject "Delete my data", or message us on WhatsApp at +91 85900 00868, from the mobile number registered with us. We will confirm once it is done.

Security

Your information is stored on secure, access-controlled systems. Staff can see only what they need for their work, and sign-in codes and passwords are never stored in plain text.

Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18 except as part of a parent's or guardian's order.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, let you know on our website or by message.
